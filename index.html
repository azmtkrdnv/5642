Android betaclosed testing

The Android build of Country Tracker is in closed testing on Google Play. Testers get it free and keep it permanently. Two minutes to join, three steps — all of them matter, so don't skip one.

01

Join the tester group

A Google Group. Use the Google account that's signed in on your Android phone.

Join group
02

Accept the test invitation

Open this on your phone and tap "Become a tester". It links your account to the test.

Open opt-in link
03

Install it

Stay on that same page — once you're a tester it shows a "Download it on Google Play" link. Tap that, then open the app a few times over the next two weeks.

Two things that break it: using a different Google account on your phone than the one that joined the group, and opening the opt-in link on a desktop. Android only — there's no iOS build. Stuck? Email azmtkrdnv@gmail.com.
